# Changelog

What changed in each version of [Photo Ctrl](https://woodwerd.github.io/photo-ctrl/), newest first. Versions are x-numbers while Photo Ctrl is a public beta.

## x0.0.1 · 2026-11-05
- Connect your own private GitHub repo for photos. A public repo is refused.
- Take a set of photos of a box, a stack or the inside of a drawer with the phone's camera, or choose them from the library, with a box ID and a note.
- On a computer or iPad, drop in an evening's photos; they group into sets by when they were taken, and can be split, merged or removed before sending.
- Every photo is shrunk to 2,048 px and cleaned of location and camera details on your device before it's sent, one commit per set.
- Box Ctrl QR codes in a photo fill in the box ID, in browsers that can read them.
- Sets wait on the device without a connection and send when it's back.
- Open a set to see what your Claude read, and tap a line to see that box or drawer outlined in the photo.
- The WOODWERD photo skill for Claude Code, with the reading and proposal formats.
